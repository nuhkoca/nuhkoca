### About

I'm an Android engineer working on the Trade Republic Android app in Berlin. I've been building Android apps since 2016. Before Trade Republic, I worked at agencies in Istanbul (Mobven and Applogist), where I shipped apps for clients such as Yemeksepeti and VakıfBank and led a team on one of the largest projects.

I'm a Udacity × Google certified Android Developer and an active open-source contributor. I care about clean architecture, testable code, and fast and reliable release pipelines.
